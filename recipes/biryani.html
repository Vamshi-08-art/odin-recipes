Chicken Biryani

Home

Description

Chicken biryani is a flavorful rice dish made with aromatic spices, tender chicken, and fragrant basmati rice.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Garlic
  • Basmati rice
  • Onion
  • Tomatoes
  • Ginger and garlic
  • Biryani spices

Steps to cook chicken biryani

  1. Marinate the chicken with spices and yogurt.
  2. Cook the onions and spices until fragrant.
  3. Add the chicken and cook until tender.
  4. Add the basmati rice and enough water.
  5. Cook until the rice is fluffy and the chicken is fully cooked.
